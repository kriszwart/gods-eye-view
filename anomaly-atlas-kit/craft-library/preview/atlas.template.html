    To scale

    Every specimen side by side at its nominal size, smallest first, next to a person 1.8 m tall. Drag to pan, scroll or use the slider to zoom, or glide the whole line.

    These are generic archetypes for visualising reported shape categories, not reconstructions of specific objects or footage. They are built from crafts.json by the craft library in this kit, and every GLB passes the Khronos glTF validator.

    Keyboard: arrow keys move through specimens, space pauses the loops, 1, 2 and 3 switch rendering modes. Each specimen has its own link, so you can share one directly.
